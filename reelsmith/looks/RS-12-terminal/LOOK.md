# RS-12 Terminal

**What it is.** A green (or amber) phosphor monitor powers on and types a log line by line; the key line flashes. Scanlines, glow, chroma bleed and corner falloff come from Lemo-Opuscar's CRT post (crt.js, MIT, vendored).

**Use it for.** Hacks, leaks, audits, "the system said...", data trails.

**Props.** title, lines [..], key, color green|amber. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: glitch at power-on, type per line, error on the key line. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it.
