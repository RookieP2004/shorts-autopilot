# RS-14 Silent Card

**What it is.** A 1920s picture-house moment: an optional still irises in on scratched, weaving, flickering film, then an art-deco intertitle holds the line. Film post, dust and hair damage, deco border and iris: Lemo-Opuscar's silent-film engine (film.js, cards.js, ink.js; MIT, vendored). Fonts: Old Standard TT and Playfair Display SC (OFL).

**Use it for.** History hooks and dates: "October 1929...", "In 1906...".

**Props.** lines [1-3; the first is the big one], level 0|1|2, image (repo path to a still), image_dur, strength (film wear 0-1). All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. It works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: projector, shutter at the cut to the card. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it.
