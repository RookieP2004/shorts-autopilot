# RS-02 Dot Map

**What it is.** A field of dots (never coastlines or borders), pins drop with a bounce, dashed routes arc between them, the camera drifts to the last pin.

**Use it for.** Routes, supply chains, where money or goods travel.

**Props.** title, points [{name, x, y, tone}], routes [[i, j, label]], tone night|paper, seed. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: thud per pin, swoosh per route. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
