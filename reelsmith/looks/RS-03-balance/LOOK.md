# RS-03 Balance

**What it is.** A brass scale: each pan fills with weights, the beam swings to the heavier side with a damped wobble, the winner's number goes gold.

**Use it for.** A vs B: interest vs inflation, cost vs benefit, which is bigger.

**Props.** title, left {label, value, unit}, right {...}, verdict. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** overlay; use backdrop: dim on bright sets. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: thud, thud, slide, hit. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
