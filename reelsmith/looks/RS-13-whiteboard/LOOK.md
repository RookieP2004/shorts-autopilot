# RS-13 Whiteboard

**What it is.** A glossy dry-erase board where boxes, arrows and labels draw themselves in marker, step by step, and the key step gets a red circle; a handwritten note closes it. Stroke engine: Lemo-Opuscar's whiteboard wb.js (MIT, vendored): hand wobble, chisel ribbons, corner overshoot.

**Use it for.** Processes and chains: how money is laundered, how a law passes, cause and effect.

**Props.** title, steps [2-5 labels], key (index to circle), note. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame; steps run top-to-bottom in Shorts, left-to-right in 16:9. It works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: marker per stroke group, pop on the circle. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it.
