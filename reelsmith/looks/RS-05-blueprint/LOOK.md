# RS-05 Blueprint

**What it is.** A technical sheet draws itself: grid paper, an exploded column of parts with a centre line, numbered callouts and a title block signed by Ved.

**Use it for.** How a thing works, what's inside, parts of a system.

**Props.** title, parts [{label, shape box|cyl|gear|circle|bolt, note}], sheet. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: paper, marker per part, pop per callout. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
