# RS-09 Word Beat

**What it is.** One word per beat, full frame, colour field flipping on each cut; the key word holds three beats and lands bigger.

**Use it for.** 2-4 s hooks, chapter punches.

**Props.** words [..], key, bpm, half, palette house|night. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: click per word, hit on the key word. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
