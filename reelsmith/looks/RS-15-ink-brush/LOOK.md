# RS-15 Ink Brush

**What it is.** Rice paper and one loaded brush: an open circle sweeps around the key word with dry-brush streaks where the ink runs out, a heavy stroke underlines it, a red seal stamps the corner. Brush engine: Lemo-Opuscar's ink-wash ink.js (MIT, vendored).

**Use it for.** One-word ideas with weight: Compound, Patience, Debt; a calm closing card.

**Props.** word, sub, seal (1-2 letters), tone ink|sepia. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. It works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: swoosh_soft on the brush, paper on the underline, stamp on the seal. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it.
