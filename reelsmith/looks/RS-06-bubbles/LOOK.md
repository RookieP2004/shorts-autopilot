# RS-06 Bubbles

**What it is.** Circles whose area is the value (honest sizes), packed without overlap, popping in small to big; the focus bubble grows while the rest dim.

**Use it for.** Shares, budgets, who has how much.

**Props.** title, items [[label, value, tone]], focus, prefix, suffix. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** overlay; backdrop: dim on bright sets. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: pop per bubble, swoosh on focus. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
