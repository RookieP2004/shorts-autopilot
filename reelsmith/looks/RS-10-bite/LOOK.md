# RS-10 Bite

**What it is.** A round plate of shares; slices settle in, then the taker slice is bitten out and slides away, leaving what's left.

**Use it for.** Tax, fees, middlemen, "what you actually keep".

**Props.** title, slices [[label, value, tone]], take, prefix, suffix, left_label. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** overlay; backdrop: dim on bright sets. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: pop per slice, stomp on the bite, whoosh. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
