# RS-07 Chalk

**What it is.** A chalkboard with ghosts of old sums; lines write themselves with dusty edges, the answer gets a chalk box.

**Use it for.** Formulas, rules of thumb, step-by-step maths.

**Props.** title, lines [..] (prefix @y: for yellow chalk), answer. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: marker per line. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
