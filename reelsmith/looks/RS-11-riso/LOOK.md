# RS-11 Riso

**What it is.** A three-ink risograph poster prints itself: blue, yellow and pink plates roll across the sheet with misregistration, halftone dots, pinholes and ink streaks; the big number overprints the sun to purple. Print engine: Lemo-Opuscar's riso.js (MIT, vendored).

**Use it for.** Rules of thumb, one big number, a poster-style title card.

**Props.** kicker, big, caption, footer, bars [values], inks classic|teal. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: slide per plate, stamp when the last plate lands. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it.
