# RS-01 Receipt

**What it is.** A till receipt prints line by line; the total rolls up and gets circled in red marker.

**Use it for.** Monthly costs, bills, "where it all goes", hidden fees.

**Props.** title, subtitle, items [[label, amount]], total, total_label, currency, note. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** overlay (paper on any set). Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: slide, type per line, register on the total, marker on the circle. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
