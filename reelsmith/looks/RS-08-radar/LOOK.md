# RS-08 Sweep

**What it is.** A scope whose beam lights each target only as it passes, leaving an afterglow, while a contact log fills underneath.

**Use it for.** Investigations, hidden networks, "spot the pattern" (dark-files).

**Props.** title, blips [{label, angle, dist, tone}], rpm, readout. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** full frame. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: riser, ding (or error for red) per contact. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
