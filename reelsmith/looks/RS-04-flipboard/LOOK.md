# RS-04 Flipboard

**What it is.** A split-flap board clacks from one number to another, digit by digit, the last one settling late; a caption flips in under it.

**Use it for.** Hook numbers, prices then vs now, countdowns to a reveal.

**Props.** from, to (or text: "1.92B"), prefix, suffix, label, tone gold|red|green, group. All optional; sensible defaults render without any.

**Placement.** overlay; backdrop: dim on bright sets. Sizes follow the layer's w/h; it works in 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 long videos.

**Sound.** Its own cues: riser, a tick per digit, hit on the last. They join the mix like any layer's sfx (peaks on the frame, no doubling).

**House rules.** Mixed in, never replacing: Ved, the town, cream cards and captions stay around it. Money stays
fictional or sourced; maps never draw country outlines or borders.
