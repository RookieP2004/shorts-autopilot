The Explainer - Channel Bible

Only the owner (Sujal) changes sections marked 🔒. The weekly review may tune sections marked 🔄 and logs every change in §12.
The repo copy (channel/memory/bible.md) is the working copy; this Google Doc is synced from it every week, and edits the owner makes here are pulled back in by the weekly review.

1. Identity 🔒
• Channel: The Explainer · @the_explainer-n3d · YouTube Shorts (Instagram Reels later)
• Niche: how the world works, explained in under a minute: science, nature, space, history, everyday "why" questions.
• Promise: "One big question, answered properly, in under a minute."
• Audience: curious people 16-34, English-speaking worldwide, strong India share.
• Language: English (Professor Ved speaks with a British voice).
• Style: fully original. A 2D flat-vector explainer world ("the vast world": the study, the open world, the cosmos) mixed with simple code-made 3D for scale, crowds and places.

2. Tone 🔒
• Three words: wise, cool, dry-witted.
• Ved talks like a smart friend who happens to know everything: short sentences, no jargon without a picture, never preachy.
• One quick, current, on-topic joke per video (§11). Never a clown, never "boomer" humour.
• Never: clickbait that isn't true, fear-mongering, politics, religion, medical or financial advice, punching down.

3. Look 🔒
• Palette, fonts, background and captions live in channel/style.json. Characters are flat vector, no outlines, two-tone shading; sets keep lines; warm key light from the upper left, cool shadows, teal/amber grade.
• Layout: visuals and headline y 0.10-0.34, captions y≈0.41, Ved y≈0.68 with h≈0.40, nothing important below y 0.78 or right of x 0.86.
• Sets: study.svg (home), world.svg (nature, history outdoors), cosmos.svg (space, physics).
• 3D: one or two low-poly shots per video (faceless people, simple places), slow camera, same light. Usually full-screen with Ved as voice-over; when Ved shares a 3D shot he stays 2D and in front.
• References: docs/sheets/professor_ved_character_sheet.png, vast_world_style_guide.png, vast_world_3d_guide.png.
• Visual-first: Ved hosts, the pictures explain. Ved is on screen for the hook, the payoff and the comic beat. The middle of every video is full-screen 2D motion graphics (diagrams, maps, labelled cutaways, animated props, counters, before/after) and simple 3D shots (scale, crowds, places, processes), with Ved as voice-over or, at most, small in a corner. Ved full-size for no more than about 40% of the runtime; every explanation step gets its own visual; something new moves on screen at least every 2-3 seconds; never a talking head explaining something we could show.

4. Cast 🔒
Professor Ved (id: ved) · host and explainer · about 25, sharp, calm, quietly funny; white shirt, top button open, sleeves rolled · voice Kokoro bm_george, speed 0.95, en-gb · poses: idle, warm, serious, explain, point, think, welcome, surprised, crossed + reaction faces deadpan, side_eye, smirk
Rule: characters are made only by characters/<id>/make.py. Colours, proportions and voice never change.

5. Sound 🔒
• Voice: bm_george.
• Music signature: ambient, D dorian, 78 bpm, seed 21, ducked under the voice, loudness -14 LUFS.
• SFX meanings: pop = new item, whoosh = scene change, ding = payoff/CTA, boom = big reveal, scratch/crickets/wah = comedy beats only.

6. Formats 🔄
• big-question · weight 0.45 · 20-35 s · Ved asks, question on screen at frame 0 → the mechanism as full-screen 2D/3D visuals (voice-over) → Ved reaction beat → payoff that answers it · hook: question
• myth-bust · weight 0.30 · 20-30 s · "MYTH:" headline + Ved deadpan → the truth shown as visuals/evidence (voice-over) → why the myth exists · hook: myth-bust
• scale-shock · weight 0.25 · 20-30 s · huge number counting up at frame 0 → make it visible in full-screen 3D (voice-over) → a comparison the viewer feels · hook: number-shock
Skeletons: jobs/_templates/<format>.json. Never the same format three days in a row.

7. Hooks 🔄
question · myth-bust · number-shock · "you've been told X" · countdown. The hook is on screen at frame 0 and spoken in the first 1.5 s.

8. Titles & descriptions 🔄
Title ≤ 60 characters, at most one emoji, true curiosity. Description: one-line summary, then "Sources:" with links. 5-10 tags, plus #shorts and 2 niche hashtags.

9. Accuracy 🔒
Every factual claim is checked with a search in the same run, with sources in meta.sources. Anything unverified is dropped.
